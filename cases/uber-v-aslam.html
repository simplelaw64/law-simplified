CASE STUDY • EMPLOYMENT LAW

Uber BV v Aslam

[2021] UKSC 5

Uber described its drivers as independent contractors. The Supreme Court had to decide whether the paperwork reflected reality.

COURT

UK Supreme Court

YEAR

2021

AREA OF LAW

Employment Law

READ TIME

3 minutes

THE CASE IN ONE MINUTE

What happened?

A group of Uber drivers argued that they were legally workers.

Uber argued they were independent contractors.

The Supreme Court unanimously held that the drivers were workers for the relevant employment legislation.

01

The Facts

Drivers used Uber's app to receive passenger trips.

Uber controlled important aspects of the service, including fares and contractual terms.

The drivers argued that the reality of the relationship was more controlled than the paperwork suggested.

THE BIG QUESTION

What matters more: what the contract says, or how the working relationship actually operates?

02

What did each side argue?

UBER

Drivers worked independently

Drivers could choose when to log in and were not required to work fixed hours.

THE DRIVERS

Their independence was limited

Uber controlled fares, terms and important aspects of access to work.

YOUR TURN

What would you decide?

Should Uber drivers have been legally treated as workers?

03

What did the Supreme Court decide?

The drivers won.

The Court looked at the reality of the relationship and the protective purpose of employment legislation rather than treating the written contract as decisive.

IN SIMPLE TERMS

The paperwork does not automatically win.

The court asked how the relationship actually operated.

THE LEGAL PRINCIPLE

Employment law looks beyond labels

Contractual wording is relevant, but it is not necessarily decisive when determining statutory employment rights.

04

Why does this case matter?

The decision matters across modern platform and app-based work.

THINKING ABOUT THE CASE

Was the decision convincing?

I think it is convincing because flexibility over when someone works does not necessarily mean they control how the work operates.

THE TAKEAWAY

If you remember one thing...

Calling someone self-employed does not settle their legal status if the reality points towards worker status.
