WHY LAW SIMPLIFIED?

Legal language shouldn't stop people from being curious.

Law is not just something you hear about in a courtroom. It is involved in businesses, workplaces, shopping, technology and many of the decisions we make every day.

In business

A company discovers that another business has broken a contract. What can it do?

A company's shareholders disagree with an important decision. What rights do they have?

In everyday life

You buy something online and it arrives damaged. What rights do you have?

You start a new job and receive an employment contract. What do all those terms actually mean?

These are legal questions, even if they do not always feel like it. This blog is about making those ideas easier to understand, one case, concept and legal word at a time.
